# Zakard Shop Refund and Return Policy

Synthetic policy corpus for the Policy & Refund Support Agent demo.
Not affiliated with any real retailer.

## 1. Refund Period

- Refund requests can be made within **7 days** of receiving the product.
- Refunds for change of mind are only accepted if the product remains undamaged.

## 2. Non-Refundable Conditions

- Refunds are not possible if the product is damaged due to customer negligence.
- Refunds are refused if product value is significantly diminished due to label removal or package opening.
- For electronic devices, refunds are not accepted after powering on the device, unless there is a product defect.

## 3. Refund Process

1. Click the **Request Refund** button in **My Page**.
2. Select the reason for the refund and attach relevant photos.
3. Once reviewed by Customer Service, the refund is completed within **3 business days**.

## 4. Contact Information

- For further inquiries, contact Customer Service (`02-1234-5678`) or use 1:1 chat support.
- Support hours: weekdays **9:00 AM – 6:00 PM** (excluding weekends and public holidays).
